# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their background,
experience level, nationality, language, gender, age, or field (developers, researchers,
students and curious beginners alike).

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Give credit to others' work, ideas and data sources.
- Accept maintainers' decisions gracefully, even when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Flooding the project with low-effort or automated (bot/AI-generated) issues, PRs or comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues/PRs, and temporarily or permanently
block anyone who breaks these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainer at
**_maintainer contact email_**. Reports are kept confidential.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). If you prefer, replace this
file with the official Contributor Covenant text.
